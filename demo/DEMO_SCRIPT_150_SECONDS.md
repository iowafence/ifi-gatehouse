## Say / do not say
* SAY: “recorded and replayed offline”, “verified by position”, “localization”, “contradicted”, “conditional / self-declared”, “provisional”, “fictional demonstration”.
* DO NOT SAY (not true): that real spatial understanding was demonstrated; that gate symbols were reliably classified; that all 32 real findings were verified (12 text facts were verified on their own document, 4 gate-label sides, 4 label positions, 1 leader); that DP-001 is bid-ready; that approvals are authenticated or durable; that a live AMD call happened.
* If asked “did the AI fail?”: “Yes — on Type B, twice, with confidence ‘clear’. That is the point: the claim was checked by code and a human decides.”
